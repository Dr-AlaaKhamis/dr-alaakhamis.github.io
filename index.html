Books

February 1, 2023
|
Books

  • Optimization Algorithms: AI techniques for design, planning, and control problems introduces the AI algorithms that can solve these complex and poorly-structured problems. Inside you’ll find a wide range of optimization methods, from deterministic and stochastic derivative-free optimization to nature-inspired search algorithms and machine learning methods.
  • Smart Mobility: Exploring Foundational Technologies and Wider Impacts gives a holistic view of smart mobility systems and services and covers how the smart mobility triad — technology, governance, and city planning — work together to create a smart and sustainable mobility.
  • AI Search Algorithms for Smart Mobility, an open-source Jupyter book.
